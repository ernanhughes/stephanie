# STEPHANIE-EVOLUTION-02

**Objective:** test whether reviewed historical experience improves subsequent engineering investigations, using the same local model in no-memory (A), lexical retrieved-memory (B), and all-reviewed-history (C) arms.

This is a bounded development study over eight sanitized mechanisms. It does not establish operational effectiveness, generalization, or recursive self-improvement. STN06 remains unchanged because it studies coding-task transfer and is not an investigation-quality experiment.

## Safety and limits

Only Stephanie source and isolated data are writable. AI is consumed through its existing inference interface; no AI changes were needed. Other repositories and operational services/databases remain untouched. The generation cap is 24 planned calls; 30 total is the hard ceiling, 120 seconds/call, no paid inference. Failed calls are retained and never retried. The model's cost remains unknown if the provider reports no price.
