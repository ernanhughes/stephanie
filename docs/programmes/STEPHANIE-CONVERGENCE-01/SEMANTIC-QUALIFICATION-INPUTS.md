# Future semantic qualification — inputs, not a preregistration

**Not frozen and not executed.** A separate study commission must approve the protocol, call budget, evaluator and data policy before any model call.

## Research question

For fresh evidence-bounded engineering investigations, does Stephanie produce a correct, useful, appropriately uncertain hypothesis and next action? Separately test whether reviewed historical experience improves those outputs. Do not conflate valid JSON with semantic success or retrieval with benefit.

## Minimum task/evaluator inputs

- Fresh development tasks not used in SE2/SE2Q, with multiple problem families, explicit supplied observations, irrelevant/noisy evidence, conflicting evidence and tasks that require abstention or request for more evidence.
- Candidate, baseline and evaluation manifest with immutable source hashes, task identities, fixed route/config/model identity, output schema, fixed context limits, time/output/cost ceilings and no retries/hidden fallback.
- Independent evidence-based evaluator with explicit dimensions: claim support, key observation coverage, hypothesis validity, uncertainty calibration, next-step value/safety, citation validity and abstention correctness. Keep independent evaluator blind to arm labels and protected answers.
- Relevant-history versus irrelevant-history controls, plus no-history baseline. Match retrieved context length or account for token/cost differences. Store retrieval results and provenance; assess relevance independently.
- Protected acceptance answers/evaluator labels excluded from prompts, retrieval, candidate generation and model-visible traces. Separate evaluator identity/data from Stephanie's process and test leakage controls before study.
- Predeclared criteria for pass/fail/inconclusive, minimum sample, per-task aggregation, missing/unknown response handling, failure classification, and whether additional human adjudication is required.
- Report structural and semantic outcomes separately; provide observed counts, intervals only if design supports them, costs and missingness. No reliability/generalization claim from a tiny development sample.

## Preservation and controls

SE2's 24 failed attempts remain historical parser failures with unknown causes. SE2Q's 1/3 semantic result remains a post-run checklist and cannot be promoted into a preregistered metric. Original SE2 tasks are exposed, not holdouts. Keep STN06's preregistration and frozen identity unchanged. A future integrated study may use Worker for execution evidence and Praxis for comparison, but only after interfaces/configuration are pinned and a human authorizes execution.
