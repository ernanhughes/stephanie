# Inputs for SC5 research

SC4 establishes a bounded Mission presentation/projection layer, not an operational experiment system. SC5 research should address:

- A governed Mission-to-Stephanie authorization and dispatch contract with explicit experiment scope, resource budgets, cancellation, and ownership.
- Worker execution-ledger availability and durable correlation across Mission, Stephanie, Worker, and artifacts.
- Signed or otherwise trusted provenance for frozen candidates, protocols, hypotheses, results, evaluator decisions, and experience records; a post-hoc hash manifest alone is insufficient.
- Raw-response retention, access, redaction, and reproducibility policy.
- Isolated end-to-end acceptance and operational packaging, backup, rollback, and activation gates.

These are research inputs only; SC5 implementation and new experiments are not authorized by this report.
